## Prompt

Vertical science fiction novel cover, 2:3, full-bleed cinematic artwork, no ornamental border.

SCENE: A cold mining valley on an alien exoplanet at dusk, seen from a low ridge. In the foreground, on black basalt rock, a large husky-like dog with a dark segmented cybernetic collar around its neck stands in profile, ears forward, breath visible in the cold air; beside the dog stands a two-metre-tall humanoid industrial robot with matte grey plating, a single amber sensor band across its face and one heavy hand resting near the dog but not touching it. Both look toward the valley below.

In the middle distance, a rough mining outpost: two tall drilling towers, pressurised domes, a half-built fuel plant, scattered amber work lights glowing through thick fog. The ground is grey ash and wet basalt; fine ash falls through the air.

Far behind, jagged black basalt needles rise out of the mist; above them hangs a thin luminous crescent, the bent light of a gravitational anomaly, and the tiny silhouette of an old 1957 satellite drifting across it. Sky in cold graphite and deep teal with pale light low on the horizon.

Mood: quiet, cold, industrial, grave; hard science fiction, used future, painterly realism, fine detail, strong contrast between cold shadows and warm amber lights.

TYPOGRAPHY, integrated into the artwork, clean sharp serif capitals, every word spelled exactly:
- At the very top, centered, small letterspaced capitals (publisher brand): MOTIVATIER CLASSICS
- Centered title in very large capitals across the upper-middle sky: DER ZWEITE ORBIT
- Author name, letterspaced capitals, across the lower area: TIMO VERITA
- Bottom, very small letterspaced capitals (legal publisher): MOTIVATIER STIFTUNG
- Keep a clear word space between every word; no other text, no barcode, no price, no watermark, no text boxes.

Safe margins: no lettering may come closer than 7 percent of the height to the top or bottom edge and 7 percent of the width to the left or right edge. The dog and the robot must not overlap the title; the title stays readable against the dark sky. No drop shadows.
